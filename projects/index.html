An open-source framework for self-improving systems. I am a core developer and maintainer. 800+ GitHub stars; used at Google, Uber and AMD.

Continuous optimization: self-evolving search

AdaEvolve (preprint) and EvoX (COLM 2026)

AdaEvolve tunes the search as it runs; EvoX lets the LLM rewrite it. Together, they beat every open framework on 96% of 196 tasks. SkyDiscover reaches 2.17× over AMD's expert-tuned SGLang.

Continuous optimization: self-evolving evaluator

DuoDiscover, integrated into Google Antigravity

Agents write fast evaluators for slow benchmarks and recheck them against the real system. Results include 1.92× faster vLLM serving, first place on 121 of 235 SOL-Exec GPU kernels, and a CPU prefetcher that beats the best human and AlphaEvolve designs (6.3% vs. 3.8%). On SWE-fficiency, DuoDiscover doubles the speedup of human experts (15.0× vs. 6.9×).
